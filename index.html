How it works

  1. 1

    Photograph your clothes

    Lay each piece flat on a plain surface and take a photo, or pick several at once. AGORA cuts out the background and reads the main colour right in your browser.

  2. 2

    Add your week

    Type in classes and plans, or import a calendar file (.ics). AGORA uses the hours you’re out, not just the day’s average temperature.

  3. 3

    Pick a look and go

    Each day you get three outfits that suit the forecast, the occasion and each other. Tap a piece to swap it. Choose one and it’s saved to your calendar.

Everything your wardrobe needs, in one place

Weather that follows your day

A live hourly forecast for your city, matched to the hours of your plans. If the evening turns cold, the look names the layer to bring. If it rains, an umbrella joins the outfit.

A closet that sorts itself

Upload up to 20 photos at once. Each piece gets its background removed, its colour named and its category set, ready to edit.

Looks that work together

Outfits are scored on warmth, formality, colour harmony and pattern clashes, then explained in plain words. Choose mannequin or flat-lay view.

Week and month planning

See each day’s weather and best look side by side, import your timetable, and find every outfit you’ve saved on the calendar.

2D try-on

Dress a mannequin with your own photos to see how pieces sit together before you commit to a combination.

Backup in one tap

Export your whole closet, plans and history as a single file, and import it on another browser or phone.

Your closet stays on your device

There’s no account to create. Photos are processed in your browser and saved there, not on a server. Only the weather request leaves your device, and it sends just your city.

Questions

Is AGORA free?

Yes. AGORA is a student project. There’s no account, subscription or payment.

Where are my photos stored?

In your browser’s storage on the device you’re using. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so export a backup from your profile first.

Which cities have live weather?

Ansan, Suwon, Seoul, Icheon, Paju, Yangpyeong and Dongducheon. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo and cover 16 days. If the forecast can’t be reached, AGORA uses an estimate from Gyeonggi climate data and tells you so.

Can I use it on my phone and my laptop?

Yes, in any modern browser. Your closet doesn’t sync between devices yet: export a backup on one and import it on the other.

What photos work best?

One piece per photo, laid flat on a plain, contrasting surface, with the whole garment in frame. Daylight helps AGORA read the colour correctly.

Why didn’t I get three looks?

AGORA only suggests outfits it can build from your closet. Add at least a top, a bottom or a dress, and shoes. More pieces give more and better combinations.
