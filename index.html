How it works

Three steps.
Then just get dressed.

  1. 1Snap your clothes

    Lay a piece flat and take a photo, or pick up to 20 at once. AGORA cuts out the background and names the colour, right in your browser.

  2. 2Add your week

    Type in classes and plans, or import your timetable as a calendar file. AGORA dresses you for the hours you’re actually outside.

  3. 3Pick a look and go

    Three outfits that fit the forecast, the plan and each other. Swap any piece with a tap. Wear one and it lands on your calendar.

Questions

Is AGORA free?

Yes. It’s a student project. No account, no subscription, no payment.

Where are my photos stored?

In your browser’s storage on the device you’re using. Clearing browser data deletes them, so export a backup from your profile first.

Which cities have live weather?

Ansan, Suwon, Seoul, Icheon, Paju, Yangpyeong and Dongducheon, with a 16-day forecast from Open-Meteo. If it can’t be reached, AGORA uses a Gyeonggi climate estimate and tells you.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes, in any modern browser. Your closet doesn’t sync between devices yet: export a backup on one and import it on the other.

What photos work best?

One piece per photo, laid flat on a plain surface that contrasts with it, whole garment in frame, in daylight.

Does AGORA use AI?

The outfit picks come from AGORA’s own scoring, which runs on your device. Two optional extras use Google Gemini: auto-tagging new photos, and a short stylist note on a look. Only the photo or outfit you choose is sent, and only when you tap the button.

Why didn’t I get three looks?

AGORA only suggests outfits it can build from your closet. Add at least a top, a bottom or a dress, and shoes. More pieces give more combinations.
